## 3. Blast-Radius-Ordered Taint-Ranking of High-Leverage `INPUT` Rows (Mission A.2)

### 3.1 The Documented Harness-Deception Class (`HW-MAP.md` lines 382–434 & 464–484)

The corpus's own history documents **six concrete cases** where a benchmark or correctness harness produced a false measurement or passed a broken kernel because the test arena coincided with the bug:
1. **Offset-0 Arena Coincidence (Errata #18 & #25, `HW-MAP.md` lines 390–399)**: The standalone S1 A-staging probe tested one 4-KiB block at S1 offset 0 (passing 8/8 bit-exact), whereas the integrated attention kernel placed `P` at S1 offset 48 KiB (`0xC000`), silently zeroing rows 2–15 (`max_abs = 0.236`). Likewise, every single-chunk `C[0]` probe was immune to Erratum #18 (`C[f]` view slices dropping offsets back to chunk 0).
2. **Low-`k` / Zero-Init Arena Coincidence (Erratum #19, `HW-MAP.md` lines 401–409)**: Standalone `f16×f16→f32` probes tested `k ≤ 256` from a freshly zeroed L0C chunk without intervening barriers or `A` re-lifts, missing the multi-call f16 accumulate corruption across `k = 4 096`.
3. **Short-Period Host Fill Aliasing (Erratum #21, `HW-MAP.md` lines 411–417)**: Period-16/17 host initialization patterns made every 128-token KV block identical and every row-16 stride equal, making `srcStride` bugs (#23), `C`-offset drops (#18), and `A`-row zeroing (#25) bit-exact against reference.
4. **Scale-1 / Tiny-Count Probe Blindness (Errata #22 & #24, `HW-MAP.md` lines 419–424)**: Testing `Muls` at `scale = 1.0` or element copies at `≤ 4 KiB` hid the silent `Muls` no-op and the `> 4 KiB` partial-landing bug.
5. **Timing-Dependent Race Hiding (Errata #28, #29, #30, #31, `HW-MAP.md` lines 426–434)**: Single-nest probes missed B-slot ping-pong carry across loop nests (#30); draining L0C before reading a vector reduction hid the 8-core scalar-readback race (#31).
6. **Host Dump-Decode Stride Bug (Retracted Erratum #14, `HW-MAP.md` lines 464–484)**: The host reader stepped by `16 384 B` instead of `1 024 B` between drained 1-KiB chunks, reading zeroedUS memory for rows 16–127 and falsely blaming silicon `k=64` `i8×i8→i32` for six rounds.
